Our Story

From a simple idea to India's trusted healthcare partner

Journey of Transforming Healthcare

Founded in 2020, QuickCare emerged from a simple observation: quality healthcare should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or economic status. Our founders, a team of doctors and technology experts, came together with a shared vision to bridge the healthcare gap in India.

What started as a small platform connecting patients with doctors in metro cities has now grown into a comprehensive healthcare ecosystem serving millions across 100+ cities. We've successfully facilitated over 1 million consultations and continue to expand our services to reach every corner of India.
